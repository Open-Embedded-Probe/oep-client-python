# Changelog / 変更履歴

## Unreleased
- (EN) port_speed follows the host guide's reference procedure (oep-spec host-development-guide.ja.md §7; core §3.5 is the handshake). `raise_speed(hst, candidates=(500000,), *, flows=None, verify=None, baseline=None, frames=16, verify_ms=None, idle_ms=3000, port=None, record=False)` replaces the old `rates, verify_bytes, verify_s, duplex_s`. The minimal form (§7.2, the default; ~50 ms, no measurement): try -> switch to the requested baud -> settle 20 ms -> confirm (100 ms x3) -> commit. The full form (`verify=True`, or `flows` given; §7.3): a baseline at the boot speed per flow (this session's frames when 60+ and under 10 %, else 60 measured per flow, re-measured at n=1 when over 10 %), then per candidate every `flows` ("in"|"out"|"duplex", n; default all three at the most the link keeps) for `frames` (16) frames at max_frame-16, a flow failing on broken+lost >= 3 and a ratio over max(2x baseline, 5 %), re-run at n=1 (then n=1 is the link's cap), one failed flow failing the candidate. `SpeedTrial` now carries `actual`, `switched`, `flows` (`FlowResult`: flow, n, frames, broken, lost, kb_s, ratio, passed), `n_cap`, `in_kb_s`/`out_kb_s`/`duplex_kb_s`; `SpeedReport` adds `verified`, `baseline`, `baseline_flows`, `step_downs` (`StepDown`), `skipped`. In use: the frames of the last 3 s (none judged under 50) over max(2x baseline, 10 %) broken or lost step the link down (was 3 strikes in 5 s); a missed answer still falls back to the boot speed within port_speed_idle_max_ms + 1 s (a link error when no confirm comes, never back to the raised rate). The keepalive interval is set under half of idle_ms. `set_baud` switches to the requested baud and to the probe's answered baud only when the OS refuses it (obligation 6/2). `oep speed` keeps working and gains `--candidates`, `--flows`, `--verify`/`--minimal`, `--frames`, `--no-record`.
- (JA) port_speed を host 開発ガイドの参考手順に合わせた（oep-spec host-development-guide.ja.md §7。core §3.5 は握手だけ）。`raise_speed(hst, candidates=(500000,), *, flows=None, verify=None, baseline=None, frames=16, verify_ms=None, idle_ms=3000, port=None, record=False)` が旧 `rates, verify_bytes, verify_s, duplex_s` を置き換える。最小の形（§7.2、既定。約 50 ms、計測なし）: 試す → 要求した baud に切り替え → 20 ms → confirm（100 ms×3）→ 決める。完全な形（`verify=True` か `flows` を渡す。§7.3）: 起動時の速さの基準を流し方ごとに取り（このセッションの 60 フレーム以上かつ 10 % 未満、無ければ 60 フレーム。10 % 超は n=1 で測り直し）、候補ごとに `flows`（("in"|"out"|"duplex", n)。既定は link が出す最大で 3 つ）を `frames`（16）フレーム max_frame−16 で流し、壊れ+失われが 3 以上かつ割合が max(基準×2, 5 %) 超で流し方は通らず、n=1 で流し直し（通れば n=1 が上限）、1 つでも通らなければ候補は通らない。`SpeedTrial` は `actual`、`switched`、`flows`（`FlowResult`: flow, n, frames, broken, lost, kb_s, ratio, passed）、`n_cap`、`in_kb_s`/`out_kb_s`/`duplex_kb_s` を持つ。`SpeedReport` に `verified`、`baseline`、`baseline_flows`、`step_downs`（`StepDown`）、`skipped` を足した。使用中は直近 3 秒のフレーム（50 未満は判定しない）が max(基準×2, 10 %) 超で降りる（旧: 5 秒に 3 回）。応答が来ない要求は port_speed_idle_max_ms + 1 秒の内に起動時の速さへ戻る（confirm が通らなければリンクの失敗。上げた速さへは戻さない）。keepalive の間隔は idle_ms の半分未満にした。`set_baud` は要求した baud に切り替え、OS が断ったときだけ probe の応答の baud にする（義務 6/2）。`oep speed` は動いたまま `--candidates`、`--flows`、`--verify`/`--minimal`、`--frames`、`--no-record` を足した。

## 0.0.26
- (EN) A request that may take long on the probe is waited for that long (core §6.1: the probe does not count the lease while it runs one): `Host.expecting(ms)` (and `request(..., expect_ms=)` / `call(..., expect_ms=)`) makes the link wait at least that and 0.5 s (`link.EXPECT_MARGIN_S`) for each answer, at any rate - so the raised-rate wait of a quarter of the lease applies to ordinary requests only. Passed down by riscv-dm run (`timeout_ms`), dmi (its wait_us and poll_us steps, `riscv.dmi_wait_ms`), attach with a reset (`hold_ms`, riscv and arm), capture start (the configured `blocking_ms`; a group's the longest of its tracks) and probe.config save (`config.SAVE_EXPECT_MS`, 2 s). Such a request that times out at a raised rate still falls back. The link's own confirms, keepalives, the step down's revert and `pump` never wait longer. Fix: the keepalive a raised link sends after 1 s of quiet now goes out before the request takes its corr (`Host.before_request`); it used to go out with a higher corr first, so the probe took the request for an old one (rejected result_lost).
- (JA) probe で長くかかりうる要求はその間待つ（core §6.1: probe は要求の実行中 lease を数えない）: `Host.expecting(ms)`（と `request(..., expect_ms=)` / `call(..., expect_ms=)`）で、link はどの速さでも応答ごとに少なくともその時間と 0.5 秒（`link.EXPECT_MARGIN_S`）待つ。上げた速さでの lease の 4 分の 1 の待ちは普通の要求だけに掛かる。riscv-dm の run（`timeout_ms`）、dmi（wait_us と poll_us の step の和、`riscv.dmi_wait_ms`）、reset 付きの attach（`hold_ms`、riscv と arm）、capture の start（configure の `blocking_ms`。組はトラックの最長）、probe.config の save（`config.SAVE_EXPECT_MS`、2 秒）が渡す。そうした要求も上げた速さで時間切れなら戻る。link 自身の confirm・keepalive・降りるときの戻す・`pump` は長く待たない。修正: 上げた link が 1 秒黙った後に送る keepalive を、要求が corr を取る前に送るようにした（`Host.before_request`）。これまでは大きい corr で先に出ていたため、probe が要求を古いものとみなした（rejected result_lost）。
- (EN) port_speed (core §3.5 items 3 and 7), after a V003 jig (classic ESP32 + CH340) passed 921600 each way alone but broke a frame every ~0.5 s under sustained duplex use, the probe reverted by itself and the session's lease lapsed: `raise_speed` adds a duplex phase after the in / out phases - link_source and link_sink (max_frame) interleaved, pipelined at the in-flight that passed, for `duplex_s` (1.0) and at least `duplex_frames` (64); a broken frame fails the rate ("broke both ways at once"). `SpeedTrial.duplex_bytes` / `duplex_kb_s` / `broken_duplex`, `SpeedReport.duplex_kb_s`, a duplex column in `to_text()`, `oep speed --duplex-s`. In use: while a raised rate is in force, the link counts broken frames and resends; 3 within 5 s (`STRIKE_MAX`, `STRIKE_WINDOW_S`) step down - port_speed step 2 at the raised rate (0.2 s, never sent again), the boot speed, a confirm, the request again there. A request at a raised rate that gets no answer at all keeps the old fallback (back, confirm, once more there), but each wait is now at most a quarter of the lease (at least 0.3 s), so it ends well inside the lease. Either way the rate is not used again in that session (`SpeedReport.stepped_down`, `.down_why`, a line in `to_text()`; `raise_speed` skips it, "stepped down from earlier in this session"). Fake: `BrokenRate(duplex=True)` breaks only while a request of `min_size` comes in with such an answer still unread (the request towards the probe, that answer towards the host), `BrokenRate(every=N)` only every Nth frame; `fake_serve --broken-rate RATE[:MIN][:in|out][:duplex][:everyN]`.
- (JA) port_speed（core §3.5 の 3 と 7）。V003 のジグ（classic ESP32 + CH340）で 921600 が向きごとには通ったのに、両方向を続けて使うと約 0.5 秒ごとにフレームが壊れ、probe が自分で戻り、セッションの lease が切れたため: `raise_speed` は in / out の後に両方向同時の確かめを足した - link_source と link_sink（max_frame）を交互に、通った同時の数で並べ、`duplex_s`（1.0）かつ `duplex_frames`（64）以上。1 つでも壊れればその速さは使わない（"broke both ways at once"）。`SpeedTrial.duplex_bytes` / `duplex_kb_s` / `broken_duplex`、`SpeedReport.duplex_kb_s`、`to_text()` に duplex の列、`oep speed --duplex-s`。使っている間: 上げた速さの間、link は壊れたフレームと送り直しを数え、5 秒の内に 3 つ（`STRIKE_MAX`、`STRIKE_WINDOW_S`）で降りる - 上げた速さで port_speed の step 2（0.2 秒、送り直さない）、起動時の速さ、confirm、要求をそこでもう一度。上げた速さで応答がまったく来ない要求はこれまでの戻り方（戻る、confirm、そこでもう一度）のままだが、待つ 1 回を lease の 4 分の 1（最短 0.3 秒）までにしたので lease の内に十分収まる。どちらでもその速さはそのセッションの間使わない（`SpeedReport.stepped_down`、`.down_why`、`to_text()` に 1 行。`raise_speed` は飛ばす: "stepped down from earlier in this session"）。偽の probe: `BrokenRate(duplex=True)` は `min_size` 以上の要求が、同じ大きさの応答が読まれないうちに来たときだけ壊す（probe へはその要求、host へはその応答）、`BrokenRate(every=N)` は N 個に 1 つだけ壊す。`fake_serve --broken-rate RATE[:MIN][:in|out][:duplex][:everyN]`。

## 0.0.25
- (EN) port_speed's idle limit (oep-spec 877cf01, core §3.5 items 5 and 6; registry synced, `port_speed_idle_max_ms` = 3000): a committed rate goes back after at most 3 s with no good frame, so a host that raised the speed and died leaves it no longer than that. The fake clamps idle_ms the same way (0 and anything longer: the maximum; 0 used to mean never). `raise_speed`'s `idle_ms` defaults to the maximum (0 or more than it: the maximum). While raised, the link sends a keepalive before a request (or a pipelined exchange, or inside a long `pump`) when it has been quiet for 1 s; `SerialLink.keep_alive()` does the same for a caller that sits idle for long (no background thread). `open_host` on a serial port (COBS) retries its first confirm for about 4 s (`OPEN_RETRY_S`, the idle maximum and a second; 0.5 s each) to wait out a rate left over, then raises TimeoutError; other transports keep their timeout.
- (JA) port_speed の黙りの上限（oep-spec 877cf01、core §3.5 の 5 と 6。registry を写し直した。`port_speed_idle_max_ms` = 3000）: 決めた速さは正しいフレームが来ないまま最長 3 秒で戻るので、速さを上げた host が落ちてもそれより長くは残らない。fake も idle_ms を同じに切り詰める（0 とそれより長い値は最長。0 はこれまで戻らないだった）。`raise_speed` の `idle_ms` の既定は最長（0 と最長より長い値も最長）。上げている間、link は 1 秒黙っていれば要求の前（並べた送り、長い `pump` の中も）に keepalive を送る。長く黙る呼び出し側は `SerialLink.keep_alive()` で同じことをする（裏のスレッドは無い）。シリアルの口（COBS）の `open_host` は最初の confirm を約 4 秒（`OPEN_RETRY_S`、黙りの最長と 1 秒。1 回 0.5 秒）繰り返して残った速さが戻るのを待ち、それでも答えが無ければ TimeoutError。ほかの経路の時間切れは変わらない。

## 0.0.24
- (EN) A serial link whose port a session holds sends a request again as soon as a broken frame arrives, instead of after the timeout: the probe sends no raw bytes on a held port (oep-core §3.4), so the broken frame was the reply, and the repeat is answered from the probe's retry table.
